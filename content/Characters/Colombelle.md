###  Lore
Viscountess Colombelle was a noblewoman and a well known duelist who would join the ranks of the Lythenlok militia after the Fiendish Plight. She was Simon's lover, and it was during these harsh times, that the couple decided to marry. Tragically however, their wedlock was short-lived, as Sorriso, Simon's apprentice, would shoot him right through the chest, as she fled the scene with Solis's tome. Devastated by the death of her betrothed, Colombelle dedicated herself to vengeance, forming [[the Tolling Procession]], to which many would congreate in honour of Simon. The mourners would go to place Simon's coffin atop a carriage which they would parade during the long nights, hunting down fiends along the way in his honour. [[The Tolling Procession]] was named as such after the large bell that accompanies the carriage bearing Simon's coffin, signifying his death, and the approaching death of his murderer. After hearing the news from Serasenza, Colombelle and the Tolling Procession would travel to Serasenza to bring Sorriso to justice.

### Relationships
#### Simon
As Simon's lover she would often scold him for his reckless heroics, anxious she would lose him. Although she partially blames herself for his behavior as she was the one who taught him combat. The two were spectacular dancers, especially on the battlefield, envied by many. Such a shame, that she can no longer dance now, bereft of her partner. 
#### Sorriso
Colombelle's nemesis, her heart is consumed by raw hatred at mere thought of Sorriso's vile grin. What irks her the most, is the fact Sorriso treats their rivalry as a mere game.
