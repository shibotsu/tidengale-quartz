###  Lore
Solis was a genius alchemist, pioneering the craft. He made many great discoveries, some forbidden, but chief among them was sparkstone, also known as the sulfur of Solis. This sulfur, found at the depths of the  Sea of Solis, was became the cornerstone of [[Lythenlok]]'s arsenal against the encroaching swarm of fiends. He mentored many talented alchemists, but his best student was [[Simon]], who after Solis passed away, would take on his role as the custodian of his knowledge.

### Personality
A wise man, respected by all, and at his burial mourned by all. He had a calming presence about him, as if everything was going to fall into place eventually, and dawn finally rise, waking all from the ongoing nightmare. 
### Relationships
#### Simon
Simon was his most promising student, and the one he entrusted his legacy to. There were still so many things he longed to teach him, but alas, his time had come to an end, far too soon for anyone's liking.
