###  Lore
Lucile was always ambitious and proud, with the wit to match. Always haughty, demanding respect and subservience, and never apologizing. Suffice to say, not all were fond of the princess, but her shrewdness could not be denied. Eventually, her ambitions outgrew her capabilities, despite her charisma and her cunning, she could not sustain her rule for long after she overthrew her father, murdering him in cold blood. She would be overthrown and jailed by the rebels, to forever rot in Valcen's darkest cell. It was only when the Fiendish Plight overrun Valcen that in the midst of chaos was she able to make her escape. After learning of the recent events, she saw this as a second chance to achieve her wish. [[Jen]] has fallen, and [[Lythenlok]] was in chaos. This was her moment to shine. The people needed a ruler, someone to rally behind, someone to reinvigorate their morale during these difficult times. Who better than her, she thought. To this end, she would make her way back home, to castle Ryal in Cettleon, where she would plot her resurgence.

### Personality
Many would believe her an entitled, spoiled brat, if it were not for her wit and charisma. Her domineering and ambitious nature might come off as arrogant, but it belies her wisdom and her ultimate goal. What everyone is certain of, is that she is ruthless in her pursuits, and will gladly sentence one to death should they dare stand in her way.
### Relationships
#### Valentin
Although Lucile and her father bore the same wish, she found his approach to achieving it far too lax. This of course was unacceptable, and had to rectified, even if meant his murder.
#### Lucrèce
The two cousins would grow close, but were worlds apart in their demeanor. She found Lucrèce's pure and idealist nature to be admirable, but not befit a monarch.
