###  Lore
The king who united the three rival city-states to form the kingdom of [[Tidengale]]. Originally from [[Lythenlok]], he grew weary of the constant struggle between the cities and decided to end it once and for all. After convincing Chikage to join him, he was able to finally subdue the stubborn and proud Serasenza, finally achieving peace and prosperity for all. However, his power-lusting daughter, [[Princess Lucile]], would eventually be the end of him.

### Personality
Valentin was a ruler beloved by his people, due to his wisdom and valour. But what set him apart from the previous monarchs, was his willingness to finally put a stop to the never-ending vexations that plagued the lands. Indeed, for a time, he managed to do precisely that. Bringing some much needed stability and prosperity for all parties involved. But ultimately, he left the world far too soon.    
### Relationships
#### Lucile
Although he and his daughter did not see eye to eye on many matters, Valentin still loved her. Which is why her betrayal stung him the most, both metaphorically, and literally. 
#### Lucrèce
Valentin believed that in due time, with the proper guidance, Lucrèce could become a monarch superior to both him and Lucile combined. Alas, fate had other plans.
#### Chikage
Valentin was always aware of her conqueror-like nature, her hunger for strength and control, which is why during the unification she was his best ally, and after, his greatest concern.
