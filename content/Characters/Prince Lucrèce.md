###  Lore
Lucrèce is [[King Valentin]]'s nephew and only viable heir, seeing as his daughter, [[Princess Lucile]], murdered him and for a brief time ruled [[Tidengale]] as a tyrant. The young prince lost his parents during Valentin's campaign to unify the three cities, and so he was taken under his uncle's wing. During that time he and Lucile grew close as cousins, but he never knew of her ambition. When she murdered Valentin, he was shocked as much as he was saddened. When the time came for him to bear the crown, he had his doubts, and partially wished for Chikage to be crowned the next monarch. However, his subjects encouraged him to stand his ground, denying them would have felt like a betrayal and a dereliction of his duties. Today the prince yet lives, but in what state, no one truly knows.

### Personality
Lucrèce was an idealist at heart, noble and delicate, adored by all. Unfortunately, his naivete would eventually cause him to fall victim to a dreadful fate.
### Relationships

#### Valentin
Having lost his family at an early age, Valentin was the closest thing Lucrèce had to father. Indeed, the two were close, and Lucrèce admired his uncle greatly.
#### Lucile
Although the two cousins were once close-knitted, it all fell apart after Lucile murdered Valentin. The act still baffles Lucrèce to this very day.
#### Chikage
His first impression of the matriarch was that of an anxious ruler not trusting anyone with the future of the kingdom. And indeed he was correct, but what he did not expect, was for her to be so selfish as to subject the entire land to a curse most wretched, simply so she could remain in control. The atrocious acts of debauchery she committed upon his flesh would eventually break the prince's spirit, leaving him in despair, not even daring to dream of rescue.
