###  Lore
Šarlota hails from the noble house of Baneblood who founded the Church and were the first to devote themselves to Balfon. As the last Baneblood, she is currently the highest ranking member of the Midnight Church(aside from Balfon), following in the footsteps of her ancestors. She would later on adopt the child of her distant relatives who have fallen ill. She would name her child Buket, as he reminded her of the ever-graceful paleblooms. With the emergence of the Fiendish Plight, the Midnight Church would once more commit themselves to their sacred duty. Šarlota would take Buket alongside her to Lythenlok first, where their hunt would begin.  

### Personality
Generally speaking, Šarlota's usually the no-nonsense sort of lady, courteous and professional. When she's drunk however, now that's a different story, that's when her silly side comes out. 
### Relationships
#### Buket
Šarlota was a strict but loving mother, nearly never having to discipline the well-behaved Buket. The way she would accustom Buket to consuming corrupt blood would be by feeding him her own blood after she had imbibed some of the corrupt.
