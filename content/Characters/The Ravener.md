###  Lore
The cerebrate known as the Ravener is came from a distant, unknown part of the cosmos. What is certain about him is the fact he is a parasite, and simple one at that. To consume, propagate and conquer. Is it for survival? Or merely the pleasure? Only the beast itself knows. It wasn't long after he landed that he encountered Balfon who followed his comet. A fierce battle between the two cerebrates emerged, and the Ravener head the upper hand, until Balfon summoned forth the moon, elevating the tide and drowning him. The Ravener's body would eventually be discovered by the people of [[Jen]], who after learning of his origin, would entomb his body in an ornate sarcophagus deep under their Citadel. After being awakened by [[Queen Chikage|Chikage]], he would merge with her, granting her his power, allowing him to spread his corruption across [[Tidengale]].
