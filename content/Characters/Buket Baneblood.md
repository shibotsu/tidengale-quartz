### Lore
Currently the youngest gene reaper of [[the Midnight Church]]. He is the adoptive son of lady [[Šarlota Baneblood]] who took him as her own when he was merely a babe, as his original parents were Šarlota's distant relatives.  Šarlota would name him as such for he reminded her of the ever-graceful paleblooms. With the emergence of the Fiendsih Plight, Buket sets out for the three cities, to hunt for corrupt blood alongside his mother.

### Personality
Humble and meek in the presence of authority, one will only see his subservient side, during the hunt however, he is an ideal example of a gene reaper, a frenzied zealot. On the other hand, as a friend, he is as loyal as they come.  

### Relationships
#### Šarlota
Buket holds his mother in the highest regard, often seeking comfort under her shadow.
