###  Lore
Simon was [[Solis]]'s best student, as well as the leader of the [[Lythenlok]] militia, who revolutionized their arsenal after [[Solis]]'s discovery of sparkstone. He was also the mentor to his ambitious apprentice, [[Sorriso]], who hailed from Serasenza. Simon made a promise to [[Solis]] to keep his knowledge safe, and to never reveal the forbidden pages documented in his tome unless absolutely necessary. Unfortunately for him, his apprentice longed for the forbidden, and would plot to abscond with the tome. It was on his wedding day, when Simon would marry Colombelle, who we had known for most of his life, that [[Sorriso]] made her move, whilst everyone was distracted by celebration and ceremony. Simon spotted her, and tried to stop her. She shot Simon in the chest right in front of everyone, and fled swiftly.

### Personality
Simon was always the type to play the hero. He simply couldn't resist committing to his reckless heroics. If it weren't for his capabilities, all of that courage, would be nothing but foolishness, no matter how noble the thought.
### Relationships
#### Colombelle
Simon and Colombelle knew each other since they were children, their love naturally flourished over the years, until they finally decide to marry.  
#### Sorriso
Simon saw great potential in Sorriso, but what concerned him was her frivolous behavior. Once he joked she would be the end of him. Never did he conceive it would come true.
