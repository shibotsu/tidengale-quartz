###  Lore
Sorriso is a talented alchemist from [[Serasenza]] who traveled to [[Lythenlok]] to study under [[Simon]], who was mentored by the lauded pioneer [[Solis]]. Sorriso longed for the forbidden knowledge hidden away inside Solis's tome, but Simon would never allow her to glean the insight hidden within, for he promised his master to keep the secrets held within safe from prying eyes. But Sorriso could not resist the allure of such knowledge and studied it in secret, even stooping to self experimentation. Naturally, this alone did not satisfy Sorriso, and further, more complex and forbidden research, remained in the custody of her mentor. Fortunately for her, Simon and his betrothed, Colombelle, would soon hold their wedding at the Parlour, leaving the secrets unguarded. After stealing Soils's tome during the wedding she would make her escape whilst everyone was busy reveling. However, she would be spotted by Simon who after realizing her intentions tried to stop her. But after coming this far Sorriso had no hesitation left. She shot Simon in the chest right in front of everyone, and fled swiftly. Sorriso would sail back to her home, Serasenza, and while on her way there she would begin to uncover the secrets of Solis's tome. Among the forbidden pages she found something which piqued her interest. Solis discovered a cerebrate corpse in a large cavern beneath Serasenza. Although the cerebrate perished long ago, its immortal heart was said to still be beating. Once [[Sorriso]] returned to Serasenza she wasted no time and made her way to the cavern where the still beating heart of the forgotten cerebrate laid. After conducting her research she would come to know that the blood which the seed-like heart produced possessed incredible healing capabilities. [[Sorriso]] would go on to announce her discovery to all of Serasenza, who would declare her their Hegemon, gaining the city's full support. Afterwards she would lead an expedition to extract the heart back to her laboratory in the city. This allowed her to give the a nourishing environment which allowed the heart to produce more of the its rosenblood, as it came to be known. This rosenblood could heal both injury and illness,  and was even able to make one immune to the corruption of a fiend's foulblood. Not to mention, it could replace all other food, as it nourished the body with all the nutrients it required. With this power, the ever ambitious [[Sorriso]] with the rest of her like-minded people would set out to reclaim [[Tidengale]] from the fiend threat and solidify Serasenza as the new capital of the kingdom. Of course, to outsiders, they were no more than gluttonous, crazed addicts, preaching of salvation, when in reality, it was no more than subjugation and decadence. It wouldn't be long before [[Colombelle]], leader of [[the Tolling Procession]], would travel to Serasenza along her followers to hunt [[Sorriso]] down.   

### Personality
Her ambition aside, Sorriso was always a lively and playful girl, rarely one to take anything seriously. Simon found this side of her both amusing and concerning. Colombelle would come to find infuriating.

### Relationships
#### Simon
Sorriso respected her master but found his stubborn withholding of the tome's secrets to be most irksome. She never wished for those events to play out as they did, but he left her no choice. She does not regret her decision but most certainly finds it pitiable. 
#### Colombelle
The two didn't interact much during their time at Lythenlok. It was only after Simon's murder that Colombelle would become obsessed with hunting down Sorriso and her revelers. Sorriso however, did not view Colombelle with contempt, but rather, as new playmate.
