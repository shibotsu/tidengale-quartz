### Act 1 Lythenlok
Jenja who is the last surviving human of Jen sets off for the city of Lythenlok, however, shortly after arriving, she is grievously wounded by a fiend. Luckily, she is rescued by a tall marionette dancer clad in silver and blue, who brings Jenja to the royal castle Ryal. There, a mysterious girl operates on Jenja to save her life. Upon waking from her long sleep, Jenja would be led by the marionette to the castle's throne room, where she would learn the identity of her savior. It was none other than the disgraced princess Lucile, daughter of the late king Valentin whom she murdered to establish her tyrannical regime, but was shortly subdued by the rebellion and imprisoned in the district of Valcen. The chaos caused by the Fiendish Plight reaching Lythenlok allowed the princess a chance at escape, and now that she is free, she plots to reestablish her rule over the kingdom, especially now during these desperate times, when people are willing to look the other way if it means their safety. However, in order to do so, she will require additional forces. Lucile reveals that during the rebellion against her, both Jen and Serasenza seized the clockhearts of her marionettes, which are their source of power. The bodies of the marionettes were stored away in castle Ryal, but without the hearts, they could not function. It is during this time that Lucile would reveal unto Jenja that her heart was wounded and that she replaced it with a clockheart, which allowed her to manipulate the alchemical metal of starsilver, just like her marionettes. Jenja, who valued her honor, would see this debt repaid. Lucile, pleased with Jenja's compliance, would waste little time and put her to work. She was to travel to Serasenza first. To do so she would need to reach the harbour found in the district of Telster. As she was about to leave the castle, Jenja was halted by the marionette dancer, who introduced herself as Marian. Marian warned Jenja that a perilous journey awaited her, and that she won't be there to save her like last time, as such, she would wish to test Jenja before she sets out. The two engage in a duel, and in due time, Jenja proves her mastery over her newly obtained starsilver abilities. Judging her as worthy, Marian allows Jenja to part ways. Upon exiting the castle, Jenja was met with the beauty and grandeur of the district of Cettleon, albeit now, there was not a soul to be found, the town was left desolate. The only living things Jenja happened upon were battle wounded fiends, most likely from their encounter with Marian. Jenja would fight her way through the fiends, until finally reaching the stairway leading downwards into Telster. However, before she descended, she would come across two young ladies, wearing luxurious dresses, clearly nobility. Upon greeting the two, the sisters introduced themselves as Atria and Venvia. The two seemed rather curious by Jenja's presence and origin, even more so after confirming she was in fact the one who the princess rescued. After the pleasantries were over, the two demanded a duel with Jenja. Although Jenja objected, the two left her no choice, both striking with their rapiers. Only After satisfying the two overly-enthusiastic twins was Jenja able to descend. Once in Telster, Jenja would soon notice the increase in fiend numbers. Wandering around the district, Jenja would happen upon an old watchman inside one of the manors. The old watchman, with lantern in hand, would introduce himself as Sherman. The two would chat for a while, mostly pertaining to the dreadful state of affairs afflicting the city. After hearing of Jenja's plans to reach the harbour, Sherman would give Jenja a key, able to unlock the gates of the city hall. Armed with knowledge and the key, Jenja set out for the city hall. Upon arriving at the city hall, she could hear the sound of battle echoing through the hallway. Eventually, Jenja would find a young watchman clad in plate armor fending off fiends. After aiding him in battle, the watchman introduced herself as Hazel. Hazel's duty demands he guard the city hall parlour, assuring Jenja she would be safe here as long as he drew breath. After her respite, Jenja would march onward towards the harbour. As Jenja drew closer and closer to the harbour, she'd be ambushed by the very same fiend who not long ago mortally wounded her. A tall, slender fiend, hunched on all fours, covered in cloth and jewelry, with an elongated, crocodilian snout. After achieving retribution, Jenja found a familiar locket around the fiend's neck. It belonged to her elder brother, Jenjou. After a moment of mourning Jenja continues on her way. On her way to the harbour, she would meet a young gene reaper of the Midnight Church, named Buket, feasting on fiend blood spilled all over the pavement. The two would become fast friends, and after hearing of Jenja's objective, Buket offered his aid to her endeavour.
