Long ago in ancient times, before the founding of [[Tidengale]], a monstrosity from a distant, unkwnon world, fell down upon the earth. It was a creature of voracity and propagation, a cancer upon this world. The arrival of this new cerebrate did not escape Balfon's gaze, the god of moon and night, cerebrate of the Midnight Church. Balfon wasted no time, and set off with haste to deal with this parasite. And indeed his judgement was correct, the new arrival was an alien cerebrate, known only as the Ravener. A battle between the two ensued, but the alien newcomer proved himself a fearsome opponent, leaving Balfon with no choice but to use his authority over the moon and beckon it closer, making the tide rise, causing a great flood, drowning the Ravener. It is this monumental battle that turned the land into an archipelago. It is many years after, that the people of [[Jen]] uncovered the remains of the Ravener from the depths, still alive, but greatly enfeebled. After discovering the Ravener's alien origins and foul nature, they sealed the cerebrate away inside an ornate sarcophagus.
