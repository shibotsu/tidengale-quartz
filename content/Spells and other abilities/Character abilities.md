### Buket abilities
(Basic moves)
En passant: Buket swings his sword diagonally upwards to the left, then the right, then swings diagonally downwards to the left, then (a bit horizontally)diagonally upwards to the right.
Penetrator: Buket commits to a heavy thrust.

(Weapon art)
Bisect: Buket dashes forward, vanishing, unleashing a series of arcane slashes in a striaght line, after which he reappears.

(Spells)
Rhapsody: Buket dashes forward with his offhand conjuring an arcane vortex which drains their blood, greatly increasing Buket's frenzy.
Cherub's gift: Tendrils surge forth from Buket's mouth, draining the opponent's blood, somewhat increasing one's frenzy. 
Frenzied chorus: Two tendrils surge forth from Buket's hand, frantically swinging up and down. 
Climax: Buket teleports into the air as arcane energy swirls around his hand, forming an orb of arcane. Buket pulnges down, crusing the orb which releases a great arcane blast.

(Passive)
Humble me: Buket recovers mana when taking damage. 
Last laugh: Buket can survive after his health reaches 0 by using his mana points to stay alive. In this state the passive ability "Humble me" is disabled, and taking damage will deplete Buket's mana points.
