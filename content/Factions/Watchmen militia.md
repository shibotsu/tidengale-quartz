The Watchmen militia was formed from during the Fiendish Plight to protect what was left of the city. Knights, soldiers, constables, hunters, all those who were ready and armed to protect their city would band together to see another day. Originally led by [[Solis]], the militia would come to welcome his apprentice [[Simon]] as their new leader, until his untimely death at the hands of [[Sorriso]].

### The Tolling Procession
After the devastating death of her betrothed, Colombelle dedicated herself to vengeance, forming [[the Tolling Procession]], to which many would congregate in honour of Simon. The mourners would go to place Simon's coffin atop a carriage which they would parade during the long nights, hunting down fiends along the way in his honour. [[The Tolling Procession]] was named as such after the large bell that accompanies the carriage bearing Simon's coffin, signifying his death, and the approaching death of his murderer. After hearing the news from Serasenza, Colombelle and the Tolling Procession would travel to Serasenza to bring Sorriso to justice.
