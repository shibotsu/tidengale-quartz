Fiends, the Whore Queen's bastard children:
Fiends are the bastard children of Lady Chikage,(also known as the Whore Queen by the faithful of the Midnight Church) and the young [[Prince Lucrèce]]. Some fiends are humanoid in appearance whilst others are more monstrous, but all bare the same pale skin and dark crimson eyes. Fiends tend to grow and mature rather fast, and will crawl away in search of fresh carrion soon after given birth by the queen. The more a fiend consumes the more powerful it becomes, likewise, if a fiend is starved, it will become frail and far less of a threat physically, however, starved fiends are known to be extremly agressive. Albeit exceptionally rare, some fiends are known to hunt down and feast on other fiends, they are, after all, selfish and gluttonous creatures by nature. These cannibalistic fiends grow larger and more monstrous than any other fiend, becoming the apex predator in their respective cities, leaving neither human or their own kin safe. Exposure to a fiend's foulblood can slowly turn one into a fiend, as it contains the small parasites commonly known as pests, [[The Ravener]]'s spawn.
