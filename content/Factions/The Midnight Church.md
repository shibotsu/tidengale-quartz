The Midnight Church used to be a grand religion since before the founding of [[Tidengale]], situated at Svetasvila the Sanctum of Rhapsody Tears. At the helm of the Church was its deity, a cerebrate known as [[Balfon the Silhouette]]. Balfon was a god of night, of moonlight and darkness, as well as communion. The faithful in accordance with their master's decree are hunters of aberrations, and as such seek corrupt blood to partake in their communion, so that they may become impervious to corruption during their inquisitions. Today unfortunately, the numbers of the Midnight Church have greatly dwindeled, and very few remain. The few faithful who do remain now travel to all three of the cities in search of corrupt blood under Balfon's guidance.

### Paleblooms
Shortly after the Midnight Church's formation, Balfon gifted the very first palebloom seeds to his followers, giving rise to the cultivation of these pure white flowers. Paleblooms are vessels of arcane energy, the more they grow, the more they produce. Many faithful carry paleblooms with them, not just as a decoration or symbol of their faith, but also as an additional source of power. Very similarly to the faithful themselves, paleblooms are invigorated by blood, growing incredibly fast when exposed to it.  

### Cherubs
Small creatures who dwell in the waters near the Sanctum of Rhapsody Tears. They resemble eel goby, but have a pair of four tentacles. The cherubs have a deep connection to the Silhouette himself, it is said that Balfon was once a mere cherub himself before his apotheosis. Balfon sends his cherubs to inhabit the bodies of his worshipers in a symbiotic relationship. The cherubs bless their host's blood, allowing them the usage of the arcane. Another role the cherubs play is that of guides, tracking corrupt blood and leading their hosts towards it, and of course, making said blood edible for their human host.  

### House Baneblood
The noble house of Baneblood was the first to swear allegiance to the Silhouette and founded the Midnight Church at their residence of Svetasvila, later becoming known as the Sanctum of Rhapsody Tears. The Banebloods and their servants served Balfon as his inquisitors, also known as gene reapers,  hunting down those with corrupt blood, but over the long years their numbers have terribly dwindled and only few remain. Today, the head of the House is [[Šarlota Baneblood]], who now alongside her adoptive son, Buket, has traveled to [[Lythenlok]], on the hunt for corrupt blood.
