The Architects were a group of alchemists who would be the first humans to set foot into [[the Dreamrealm]]. It is here that they would discover jadeglass, a wonderous alchemical mineral which they would come to use as their primary source of energy, allowing them to create machinery previously thought impossible. The Architects would eventually settle in the region known as the Silent Abyss, building a majestically grand keep known as [[Arganon]]. It was during this time that the Architect's caste system was established. There existed three castes: the Choir, the Curators and the Arbiters. The most exalted of the higher ranked would form the upper echelons of the Architects, known as the Council of Sculptors. For a time, the Architects would thrive in this silent corner of [[the Dreamrealm]], until the echo of their song and toil awakened something deep within the abyss. It was a long dormant cerebrate that the Architects awakened from its slumber, Razh'gatarr, the abyssal dreamer. The Architects would face war against the cerebrate and its abyssal spawn, during which many would be sacrificed for victory over the monstrosities. Razh'gatarr was captured and imprisoned deep within [[Arganon]]. Ever since then, the Architects would occasionally need to engage the abyssal spawn which have been awakened, and now lurk the dark waters. The Council would seek a myriad of methods to put an end to the imprisoned cerebrate, but would eventually give up after the innumerable sacrifices made during the attempts, shifting their attention on expansion and security above all else.   
### The Choir
Members of the Choir make up the artists of the Architects' ranks. They are trained in various aspects of artistry, however, as their name suggests, their primary focus is on song. The song of the Architect's Choir is a fundamental part of [[Arganon]]'s culture, a grand tradition which has inspired a sense of dignity, belonging and duty in the hearts of the Architects. The Choir's song can often be heard echoing through [[Arganon]]'s halls, motivating all to serve their calling with pride. The high ranking members of the Choir are known as the Conductors, they serve as the teachers to the younger generations, as well as preachers of the Architect's values.
### The Curators
The Curators are the engineers and mechanics of the Architects. Their duties are that of construction, repair and innovation. The lower ranking are often tasked with the labor of maintaining and expanding [[Arganon]]'s facilities, as well as constructing various machinery and forging armor and weaponry. The high ranking members of the Curators are known as the Custodians, they are the designers behind the Architect's numerous inventions, otherwise, they overlook the numerous facilities of Arganon, making sure all is in order.
### The Arbiters
The Architect's Arbiters are charged with the duty of maintaining law and order of [[Arganon]]. Their tasks mostly comprise of security work, be it patrolling the hallways or escorting their peers outside of [[Arganon]]'s walls. Of course, when battle is imminent, they are the first to engage the threat. The higher ranking members of the Arbiters are known as the Administrators, their duties shift to that of judges and bureaucrats. 
### The Council of Sculptors
The Council of Sculptors is formed by three members, each the highest ranking in their respective caste. The Sculptor representing the Choir is the eldest of the council, Volkoloss, renowned virtuoso whose booming voice leads the rest of the Choir in their song. The Sculptor representing the Curators is the second eldest, Azula'tann, forgemaster and mastermind behind all of the Architect's creations, from the sturdy pillars and walls, to the swift rocket-trains. The Sculptor representing the Arbiters is the youngest, Eyinir, [[Arganon]]'s aegis, and the Architect's mightiest warrior and impartial judge.
