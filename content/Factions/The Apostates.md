The apostates are one of the main reasons [[the Midnight Church]]'s numbers have dwindled so greatly. They are former member of the Church who betrayed their fellows, led by the four Luminaries. Before they were known as the Luminaries, the quartet chanced upon an alchemical substance they named etherhaze. This substance had the power to create synthetic life. With the etherhaze, they created myriad homunculi of various species, some even completely new, harvesting and transplanting their organs onto themselves to acquire their traits. This practice went against the core principles of [[the Midnight Church]], to keep humanity's genome pure. The apostates were excommunicated and after a bitter conflict, banished to the Tumultuous Ether, from where the etherhaze originates. The Tumultuous Ether was a region of [[the Dreamrealm]], a barren land surrounded by a nebula of etherhaze, fractured to pieces by the never ending ether-storms and meteorites produced from this nebula, leaving the broken pieces of land to float in the great expanse of [[the Dreamrealm]]. Over the long years of their banishment, the apostates would learn to survive in the harsh environment of the Tumultuous Ether, building ether-foundries, facilities with which they would harvest the ether-storms in order to power their other machinery. Like their garden-domes, where they could safely use the etherhaze to grow their synthetic flora and fauna. And of course, the fortress of [[Brillagesh]], the apostate's headquarters. Eventually, the apostates managed to create gateways to travel back to Tidengale, however, they can stay open for very short periods of time, as such, they use them wisely to collect precious specimens. The apostates wish to one day return to Tidengale in full, and with their alchemy create a deity of their own, to overthrow Balfon and [[the Midnight Church]].
