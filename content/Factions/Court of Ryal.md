After Lucile escaped Valcen in the midst of chaos of the rampaging fiends, she would follow river Ryal back to Cettleon, and her home, castle Ryal. There she would eventually manage to find her old creations, her clockheart marionettes. Lucile would repair and reactivate them, reviving her steadfast army. Lucile would declare unto [[Lythenlok]], and [[Tidengale]] as a whole, that the rightful monarch has returned, and in time all will genuflect before their empress. 
### The Marionettes
The marionettes are automatons created by Lucile in order to enforce her rule. After Lucile's defeat, the marionettes were stripped of their cores and thus deactivated, stored away in one of castle Ryal's chambers, awaiting the return of their mistress.
### Clockhearts
The clockhearts are Lucile's most ingenious creation, using starsilver as its source of power. The clockhearts are generally used as cores for Lucile's marionettes, however, Lucile would design it so it could be also used as a prosthetic human heart, allowing one to manipulate the starsilver same as her marionettes. Lucile made one specially for herself, the regal clockheart, which possessed authority over all other clockhearts, granting her the power to manipulate the movement of all other clockhearts, be they inside a marionette, or human.
### Starsilver
Starsilver is the alchemical metal Lucile discovered during her youth. As the name suggests, the starsilver is harvested from fallen meteorites, and is the source of power of her clockhearts. Lucile was both a skilled alchemist as well as artificer, rivaling even the likes of [[Solis]].
