After [[the Architects]] achieved mastery over jadeglass they would develop many new inventions, achieving great technological advancement. This would lead them to explore the various regions of [[the Dreamrealm]], and eventually, they would grow curious of what lied at the bottom. At the bottom of the realm, they would discover the black ocean known as the Silent Abyss. The abyss appeared to be bottomless, and completely still, without anything in sight. It was the perfect location for them to construct their new home, isolated from the world, where they could further develop their work unimpeded. And so it is that over the long years of construction, the keep of Arganon would be complete, and [[the Architects]] could finally commence their labor. Even to this day, [[the Architects]] work to further expand Arganon's halls.
