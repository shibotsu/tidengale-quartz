Lythenlok is the largest and most developed city in [[Tidengale]], as well as the capital, used to be ruled over by the wise king Valentin before his demise and the rise of the Fiendish Plight. Today, the populace is scattered and a multitude of factions strive to achieve their own goals. The MIdnight Church seeks to eradicate blood of the corrupt. Luminary Sorriso, chief alchemist of Serasenza, seeks to take over Jeichester whilst in this state of chaos. And princess Lucile who plots to reastablish her rule as the rightful sovereign of Lythenlok. During all this chaos, the militia struggles to protect the citizenry, who have either joined one of the factions or are trying to survive on their own avoiding the conflict between the major players. Lythenlok is comprised out of three districts; Cettleon, Telster, and Valcen. For years Lythenlok has suffered from the Fiendish Plight which rose from [[Jen]]'s regal palace, the Citadel. Today, the currency in use is the Lythenlok mira, named after the first queen from the royal lineage, queen Mira, who introduced the currency shortly after her coronation. 

### Telster
After the Fiendish Plight became unleashed upon the city, it was the district of Telster that became the bulwark against [[the fiends]]. It is here that Lythenlok's genius alchemist Solis pioneered and weaponized sparkstone(also known as the Sulfur of Solis) through his craft to combat the first outbreak. After Solis's passing it was his apprentice, viscount Simon, who was considered Lythenlok's most renowned alchemist and revolutionary, accepted by most as their leader. As one of the very first alchemists, Simon strictly adhered to the ideals established by Solis, much to the dismay of his appreantice, Sorriso, who herself was an exceptional alchemist. As loyal as Simon was to his master's teachings, he would never allow Sorriso to delve into knowledge of forbidden alchemy, declared sacrilegious by the pioneers. But Sorriso could not resist the allure of such knowledge and studied it in secret, even stooping to self experimentation. Naturally, this alone did not satisfy Sorriso, and further, more complex and forbidden research, remained in the custody of her mentor. Fortunately for her, Simon and his betrothed, Colombelle, would soon hold their wedding at the Parlour, leaving the secrets unguarded. After stealing Soils's tome during the wedding she would make her escape whilst everyone was busy reveling. However, she would be spotted by Simon who after realizing her intentions tried to stop her. But after coming this far Sorriso had no hesitation left. She shot Simon in the chest right in front of everyone, and fled swiftly. Devastated by the death of her betrothed, Colombelle dedicated herself to vengeance, forming the Tolling Procession, to which many would congregate in honour of Simon. The mourners would go to place Simon's coffin atop a carriage which they would parade during the long nights, hunting down fiends along the way in his honour. The Tolling Procession was named as such after the large bell that accompanies the carriage bearing Simon's coffin, signifying his death, and the approaching death of his murderer. Sorriso would sail back to her home, Serasenza, and while on her way there she would begin to uncover the secrets of Solis's tome. Among the forbidden pages she found something which piqued her interest. Solis discovered a cerebrate corpse in a large cavern beneath Serasenza. Although the cerebrate perished long ago, its immortal heart was said to still be beating. Armed with this knowledge, Sorriso would return to her home in Seraseza where she would begin to conduct her research in secret.

### Cettleon
The district of Cettleon is unlike any other, its inhabitants are extremly wealthy, for even serveants here dress in what would be considered finery elsewhere, and most buildings share the immaculate architecture of great cathedrals, a befitting trait seeing as Valentin's royal lineage traces its origins here. Today there are very few people left in Cettleon, leaving it desolate with the exception of princess Lucile and her loyalists. At the very top of Cettleon rests the royal castle Ryal, home to the royal lineage and now Lucile's bastion.

### Valcen
The district of Valcen is Lythenlok's prison district. This is where princess Lucile was imprisoned after her short-lived tyrannical rule. Once the Fiendish Plight took over the city, Valcen was the first to be overwhelmed. In the ensuing chaos, many convicts would escape from their cells. Most were recaptured by the militia, who in their desperation to fend off the fiends, would bind them in shackles and strap bombs to the convicts' backs, forcing them to run into swarms of fiends and detonate the explosives. Lucile managed to avoid this fate, making her way to Cettleon, where she would begin her plot to reclaim her city and eventually, kingdom.
