The fortress of Brillagesh is the headquarters of [[the Apostates]], built over their long years trapped within the harsh environment of the Tumultuous Ether. To sustain Brillagesh and all the other facilities, [[the Apostates]] had to first build the ether-foundries in order to supply the facilities with the necessary etherhaze to power them. Brillagesh is connected to all of the other Apostate facilities spread across the region allowing for safe traversal between them. It is within Brillagesh itself that [[the Apostates]] conduct their experiments, they capture a variety of specimens to extract their essence and then recreate a homunculus version of it using etherhaze inside the incubator chamber. Most of the animal homunculi are sent to the garden-domes to grow whilst the human homunculi are sent to the academy, where they are taught in preforming menial tasks for [[the Apostates]]. However the unlucky among the homunculi, be they human or animal, are harvested for their organs so that [[the Apostates]] may further enhance their bodies.
