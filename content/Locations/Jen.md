The eastern jewel of [[Tidengale]], a once prosperous nation now reduced to a den of parasites. The city is divided into three districts, the Spires, with their grand gardens, the Undercity which connects the entire city, and the regal Citadel, where [[Queen Chikage]] resides. Although the city retains most of its grandure, it is now solely roamed by fiends, with the sole exception of prince Lucrèce, kept captive as the queen's mate.
