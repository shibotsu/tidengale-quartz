The western city of Serasenza is home to [[Tidengale]]'s most ambitious, with an ego to match that of [[Lythenlok]]. The city is known for its revelry, with its carnevals, casinos and fine wine. Out of the three cities, Serasenza has the least ammount of laws. Once [[Sorriso]] returned to Serasenza she wasted no time and made her way to the cavern where the still beating heart of the forgotten cerebrate laid. After conducting her research she would come to know that the blood which the seed-like heart produced possessed incredible healing capabilities. [[Sorriso]] would go on to announce her discovery to all of Serasenza, who would declare her their Hegemon, gaining the city's full support. Afterwards she would lead an expedition to extract the heart back to her laboratory in the city. This allowed her to give the a nourishing enviroment which allowed the heart to produce more of the its rosenblood, as it came to be known. This rosenblood could heal both injury and illness,  and was even able to make one immune to the corruption of a fiend's foulblood. Not to mention, it could replace all other food, as it nourished the body with all the nutrients it required. With this power, the ever ambitious [[Sorriso]] with the rest of her like-minded people would set out to reclaim [[Tidengale]] from the fiend threat and solidify Serasenza as the new capital of the kingdom. Of course, to outsiders, they were no more than gluttonous, crazed addicts, preaching of salvation, when in reality, it was no more than subjugation and decadence. It wouldn't be long before [[Colombelle]], leader of [[the Tolling Procession]], would travel to Serasenza along her followers to hunt [[Sorriso]] down.
