The kingdom of Tidengale was founded by king Valentin who united the three rival cities. The central city of [[Lythenlok]], the western city of [[Serasenza]] and the eastern city of [[Jen]]. During his rule the kingdom thrived, however, he was murdered by his power lusting daughter, princess Lucile, who would be condemned to a lifetime of imprisonment in Lythenlok's prison district of Valcen. This left Valentin's nephew, the young prince Lucrèce to become the next in line. During this time, a dispute erupted between Lady Chikage, the matriarch of [[Jen]], and the young prince. Chikage claimed she should be crowned as monarch for she was the eldest in line of succession as well as the most experienced and accomplished among the royal candidates. On the other hand, the proud and vain people of Lythenlok believed Lucrèce to be the rightful ruler due to his bloodline as well as the fact that after the unification of the cities, Lythenlok became the capital. This drove the matriarch to send out her agents to abduct the prince, and seize the throne by force. But the people of Lythenlok became infuriated by this action, leading to a civil war between the two cities. In order to win this war, Chikage chose to uncover the forbidden sarcophagus sealed away deep under [[Jen]]. And indeed, after the Margrave forced open the sarcophagus a twisted figure did emerge from the shadows, a dreadful yet frail creature, claiming to be a cerebrate known only as the Ravener. The Ravener offered Chikage a pact. The Ravener was a cerebrate with a simple goal, to consume, propagate and conquer, but he could not achieve this alone, as such, his goals and that of the queen aligned. And so, Chikage allowed herself to become a vessel for the Ravener's overflowing vitality, becoming the very first fiend. With her newly aquired power, she possessed wanton, unbridled fertility, and made sure to make use of it by copulating with the young prince, giving birth to the fiends, [[Queen Chikage]]'s vile progeny, who would thirst to consume and defile. And so the fiends would swarm [[Jen]] first, and Lythenlok right after, in search of fresh blood to feed upon, this marked the beginning of the Fiendish Plight.
