A dimension beyond the reality of the "Waking world", named the Dreamrealm after its fantastical regions, many of which question whether the laws of physics apply to this realm. No one truly knows how vast the Dreamrealm truly is, or if there even is an end to it, nevertheless, a number of regions have been explored thus far. Such as the Tumultuous Ether and the Deafening Void.

### The Silent Abyss
At the bottom of the Dreamrealm, rests a vast expanse of pitch black water, a dark ocean of nothingness. This region was named the Silent Abyss, as indeed, there is nothing to be found there, other than silence and darkness. But should delve deep enough into its abyssal depths, one will eventually come across things that should not be. [[The Architects]] saw the dark expanse as the perfect place to settle, with nothing to disturb them. Ironically, it was them, who would come to disturb the original denizens of the abyss. The abyss seemed bottomless, and many an Architect wondered if there in fact was a bottom all the way down in the depths, but after their encounter with the abyssal aberrations, they no longer wished to know.  

### The Tumultuous Ether
The Tumultuous Ether is one of the least hospitable regions in the Dreamrealm. It is enveloped by a nebula of etherhaze which produces thunderstorms, dust storms and meteor showers. Over the years, the land has become fractured, leaving the broken pieces of land to float in the great expanse of the Dreamrealm. The purple desolate wastes of the Tumultuous Ether are harsh and support little life, the fauna that remains has been altered due to the continuous exposure to etherhaze, and now mostly feeds on ether-crystals.
